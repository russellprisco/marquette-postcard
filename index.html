Questions

Good to know

What is the free mockup?

I design your ad for free, using your logo, your offer and your info, so you can see exactly how it will look on the card. You can ask for changes, and you only pay once you are happy with it.

How do I get a mockup or check my category?

Tap Request a free mockup, or text me your business name and category. I will check that your category is open and get your ad started.

Is the $200 off permanent?

No. This is our grand opening special for Marquette, and it is only for this first card.

Will it be 11x14 or 9x12?

That depends on how many advertisers sign up. Either size is enormous and stands out in the mailbox over any other piece of mail they get. It is printed in full color on both sides, and your ad size, your price and the number of homes mailed stay the same.

How many ads are on the card?

The 11x14 card has 41 spots, and a 9x12 has 23. With a mix of ad sizes there are usually about 25 businesses on an 11x14 card.

What if my category is taken?

Text me anyway. I will put you first in line if it opens up, or for the next card.

Who designs my ad?

I do, and design is included. You can also send your own design. Usually I only need your offer, and I can pull your logo and info from your website or social media.

Do I need a coupon?

Not required, but an offer works best. Something like "mention this card for 10% off" brings in new customers and makes it easy to see what the card is doing for you.

How do I pay?

Cash, check or credit card. Checks are payable to Support the UP. Your spot is locked in once payment is received. Payment plans are available through Afterpay, with payments over 6 weeks and zero interest.
